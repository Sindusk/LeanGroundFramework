# Why the steps say what they say

The incident behind each bracketed id in the stage files, under that file's name. Never loaded to follow a step; read the entry for one step only to challenge it. A retro that turns a lesson into a step adds its entry here and only a bracketed id to the step (`retro.md`).

The kit's first entries come from the projects it was extracted from: `AZ-` ids from Azothos (its lessons file and az-package skill), `PF-` ids from Polarity Fracture (its pf-package skill's why file), `MM-` ids from Metallic Meridian (its working agreements and lessons). This project's own entries use its own prefix.

## SKILL.md

- **Recipes and needed seams [AZ-141]:** Azothos's package procedure ties both rules to AZ-141: use the recipe for a common addition rather than a past package's diff, and check each novel seam a block plans against what already holds the data. The incident is in Azothos's delivery record for AZ-141.
- **Sized from the block, seams written once [PF-539]:** packages of 15 to 20 files cost $7 to $15 as one session, and the smallest dispatched chunk cost $3.64. Azothos's earlier trigger, dispatching past 200k of context after reading, fired once and its build cost more than an inline one. An audit of ten manager sessions found 16k to 25k of each opening was code bodies the chunks then read again.
- **Inline under about twelve files [PF-523, PF-540]:** PF-523's seams were 10 files, and three auditors' 211k tokens bought what one search and a few excerpts did. PF-540's audit beside its predecessor's build commit missed two of seven targets and found nothing the commit's file list did not. Azothos's one-Grep threshold for a Haiku seam map had not run for nine days of packages when its retro retired it.
- **The hard stop [PF-533]:** "about 25" calls was not one: an audit took 40 calls and 76k tokens, and still missed two seams its chunk found.
- **Sonnet for a row-to-field audit [PF-633]:** a Haiku audit answered "none found" for 10 of 18 rows whose fields existed; the manager re-read about 15 files.
- **Checking reports [PF-520]:** a check of the audit searched two of the three source trees, and a helper reading the old list reached review.
- **Windows over 40 lines [PF-542]:** an opening printed a 90-line window and parts of three more files, though the rule was already written; the plan cited a few names from each.

## build.md

- **Needed seams [AZ-141]:** as under SKILL.md.
- **Falsifications per package [PF-541]:** the project asked for three to five falsifications per package, but each chunk was asked for three to five, so one package ran about a dozen, each a red run and a green run.

## cut.md

- **The rule attacked first [PF-537]:** three rule gaps each cost a mid-build correction, and a later review found three more where the rule met another system.

## handover.md

- **Unique markers [AZ-161]:** one round and a relaunch were lost to a fixture marker (a seal colour) that older fixtures also handed out, so a stale item in the tester's long-lived world gave a wrong read-out.
- **Sizes and values worked out [AZ-177]:** one round and a relaunch were lost to a screen sized 270 high when the real window gave 252; two steps besides expected results the rules did not give.
- **Reachable conditions [AZ-183]:** a planned check needed a state that never occurs in play, and a step was lost to a text width guessed rather than measured against the longest real name.
- **No live log watch [AZ-191, MM-145]:** the manual check was 29% of a package session's cost, and AZ-191's alone $4.68 over four relaunches, while a watched log made each of its events a whole turn. MM-145's watch woke the agent for a turn per command the owner typed. Both projects reversed the watch: launch, end the turn, read the log on the report.
- **No reply to an exit notice [AZ-209]:** the owner wanted no reply at all to a launched app's completion notice, where the rule had allowed one line.

## dispatch.md

- **Produced as a list [PF-533]:** a chunk produced one value where the design allowed several, and was resumed to make it a list; names produced and consumed must match exactly.
- **Two site lists [PF-535]:** a chunk walking two site lists ran out of budget at 169k with no test written.
- **Exceptions name their effects; shared types [PF-536]:** "no zone effect lands on an ally" removed an ally's intended gain, found only at the manager's check; a chunk's added union member broke readers it did not list. Its "Read first" lacked the wiring points, so the chunk spent 30 calls before its first edit finding what the manager had found in two greps.
- **Named tests; DONE past budget [PF-537]:** "with witnesses" left tests unwritten across two chunks; a chunk returned DONE at 139 calls, and a budget nobody reads back is not one.
- **No cap, no shared-shape exception [PF-627]:** across 181 first chunks, 34 ran past 120 calls at $3.90 each against $1.51 for one under 90, 27% of chunk spend, and 26 finishers followed. Three chunks accepted at S 2 to 3 because they shared a shape ran 134, 168 and 153 calls; one left unsplit to stay at four chunks ran 130. A K 0 chunk under the exception ran 108 calls and 152k.
- **What counts as a site list [PF-632]:** a ward read by three families was tallied S 1 and ran 180 calls; chunks adding four and five production fields ran 155 and 135.
- **About four tests with K ≥ 1 [PF-541]:** three chunks at or under six tests each ran past 120 calls (134, 145, 139); the largest files' cold-start reading alone was near 60k.
- **Fast and slow tiers apart [PF-543]:** seven tests plus two browser cases in one chunk ran past 150k.
- **One check, one chunk [PF-599]:** a row planned a chunk after its partner broke the consistency check joining them, so neither chunk could go green alone.
- **The harness's count; declarations by name [PF-541]:** a chunk reported about 113 calls and the harness 134; each cold start spent 13 to 30 calls reading files where a by-name lookup prints only the body needed.
- **Fresh over resumed [PF-534, PF-553]:** chunks resumed at 106k to 300k of context re-read the whole context on every call; a fresh chunk briefed on the remainder cost less.
