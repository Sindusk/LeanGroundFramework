# {{PROJECT}} — Design History

**What this is.** The record of why `DESIGN.md` says what it says. When a decision is made, argued, reversed or completed, the verdict goes into the design and the reason comes here, so the design reads as the current rules rather than a changelog.

**What it is not.** It is not authoritative and holds no requirements. Where this file and `DESIGN.md` disagree, the design wins.

**How to add to it.** One dated row per decision, newest last: what was decided, and why. Cite the design section the rule now lives in, and the question number when a question decided it. Agents add rows with `python tools/docs.py history add <file>`, never by reading this file.
